## Who I am

I'm A. J. A. Jubayer Talukder, an undergraduate in Electrical and Electronics Engineering (Communication and Signal Processing) at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), Dhaka. I work across the stack from silicon to software — VLSI/RTL design, embedded systems and robotics, up through computer vision and large language models.

## What I work on

My ongoing thesis builds a real-time wearable assistant that decides *when* to interrupt a conversation and *what* to say, using a two-stage LLM pipeline. Alongside that, I've worked on autonomous drones, obstacle-mapping robots, deepfake detection, medical image analysis, and a full RTL-to-GDS VLSI design flow. See [[projects]] for the full list.

## Current experiments

I'm currently exploring LLM routing (cheap local models handling easy queries, escalating only hard ones to larger remote models) and compact adapter-sharing decoders for on-device inference — see [[projects/hybrid-router-vinci-monsoon|Hybrid Router]] and [[research/proactive-conversation-assistant|Proactive Conversation Assistant]].
